Teaching Philosophy

Whether I'm teaching principles of leadership, personality traits, or the psychology of selfhood, everyone who walks into the room already has a lifetime of lived data. My job is to give students the conceptual tools to examine that material. When a student realizes that a theory they just learned describes something they have been experiencing for years—that's the moment I'm after. Good teaching is good leadership, and for me that means a focus on students' personal growth and an emphasis on human connection.
